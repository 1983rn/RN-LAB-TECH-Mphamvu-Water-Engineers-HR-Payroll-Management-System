# Mphamvu Water Engineers HR & Payroll Management System 
## First-Time User Guide

Welcome to the Mphamvu Water Engineers HR & Payroll Management System! This guide is designed to help you log in for the first time, secure your account, and start using the core features of the system.

---

### Step 1: Your First Login
When you first access the application, you must log in using the default Administrator credentials that were generated when the system was deployed.

1. Navigate to the Login Page.
2. Enter the default administrator username: **`Mphamvuwaterengineers`**
3. Enter the default password: **`.org.ulandaduwe/2026/**?`**
4. Click **Login**.

### Step 2: Mandatory Password Change
For security reasons, the system requires you to immediately change the default password after your first login.

1. You will be redirected to the "Change Password" page.
2. Enter the default password again in the **Current Password** field.
3. Create a strong, new password (mix of letters, numbers, and symbols) and enter it in the **New Password** field.
4. Confirm your new password.
5. Click **Change Password**. 
6. You will now be redirected to the Main Dashboard.

---

### Step 3: Navigating the Dashboard
The Dashboard is your main control center. Here, you can see a high-level overview of your business operations:
* **Total & Active Employees**
* **Total Clients**
* **Pending Quotations**
* **Approved Contracts**
* **Total Transactions**

---

### Step 4: Adding Your First Employee
To use the Payroll and Attendance features, you first need to register your employees.

1. On the sidebar menu, click on **Employees**.
2. Click the **Add Employee** button.
3. Fill in the employee's details:
   * **Personal Info:** Name, ID Number, Gender, Date of Birth.
   * **Job Details:** Department, Designation, Basic Salary.
   * **Bank Details:** Bank Name, Account Number (required for payroll processing).
4. Save the employee profile. The employee is now active.
*Tip: You can generate an ID Card for the employee directly from their profile page.*

---

### Step 5: Managing Attendance
The system tracks employee presence to accurately calculate payroll.

**Using AI Face Recognition:**
The system uses an advanced AI face-landmark model to verify employee identities securely (anti-spoofing).
1. Go to **Attendance** > **Mark Attendance**.
2. Have the employee look at the camera to clock in or out.

**Manual Attendance:**
1. If an employee cannot use the camera, you can manually mark them present or absent from the **Attendance Log** page.

---

### Step 6: Processing Payroll
At the end of the month, you can generate payslips automatically based on attendance and loans.

1. Go to the **Payroll** tab.
2. Click **Process Payroll**.
3. The system will automatically calculate:
   * **Basic Salary**
   * **PAYE Tax** (Based on Malawi Revenue Authority brackets).
   * **Loan Deductions** (If the employee took an advance/loan).
   * **Net Salary**.
4. Once processed, you can download a **PDF Payslip** for the employee, which includes a QR code for authenticity verification.

---

### Step 7: Managing Loans & Advances
If an employee requests an advance on their salary:
1. Go to their **Employee Profile**.
2. Issue a new Loan/Advance, specifying the amount and the repayment period (in months).
3. The system will automatically deduct the monthly installment from their salary during the Payroll Processing step until the balance is zero.

---

### Step 8: Creating Quotations & Invoices for Clients
The system also helps manage your client billing.

1. First, register a client under the **Clients** tab (or directly when making a quotation).
2. Go to **Quotations** and click **Create Quotation**.
3. Add the items, quantities, and prices. The system calculates totals automatically.
4. Generate the PDF.
5. Once a Quotation is accepted by the client, you can easily convert it into an **Invoice** or **Delivery Note** from the Finance tab.

---

### Step 9: Document Verification (Security)
Every PDF generated by this system (Payslips, Quotations, Invoices, Delivery Notes) contains a unique **QR Code** at the bottom.
* Anyone (clients, banks, or auditors) can scan the QR code with their smartphone camera.
* They will be linked to a secure page on your system that instantly verifies whether the document is authentic and has not been tampered with.
